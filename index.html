Haoxiang Ren

I am a master's student in the LAMDA Group at the School of Artificial Intelligence, Nanjing University, advised by Prof. Yang Yu. I started my master's studies in 2026, after completing my undergraduate studies at the same school (2022–2026).

My research interests lie in embodied intelligence, world models, and reinforcement learning. I am interested in how world models can support efficient policy learning and reliable decision-making for robots.
